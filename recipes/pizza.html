Pizza

Description

Pizza is a savory dish of Italian origin consisting of a usually round, flattened base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and often various other ingredients baked at a high temperature, traditionally in a wood-fired oven.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups warm water (110 degrees F/45 degrees C)
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 2 1/4 teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 3 1/2 cups bread flour
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1/4 cup cornmeal
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup sliced pepperoni
  • 1/2 cup sliced black olives
  • 1/2 cup chopped onions
  • 1/2 cup sliced mushrooms
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. In a small bowl, dissolve sugar in warm water. Sprinkle yeast over the top, and let stand for about 10 minutes, until foamy.
  2. In a large bowl, combine flour and salt. Make a well in the center, and add the yeast mixture and olive oil. Mix well and form into a ball. If the dough is sticky, add more flour. Knead the dough until smooth, about 7 minutes. Lightly oil a large bowl, place the dough in the bowl, and turn to coat with oil. Cover with a damp cloth, and let rise in a warm place for 25 minutes.
  3. Preheat oven to 450 degrees F (230 degrees C). Punch down the risen dough on a floured surface. Roll out into a 12-inch circle. Transfer to a lightly greased pizza pan. Spread with pizza sauce. Top with desired toppings, such as pepperoni, black olives, mushrooms, and onions. Cover with cheese.
  4. Bake in preheated oven for 15 to 20 minutes, or until golden brown. Let baked pizza cool for 5 minutes before serving.
